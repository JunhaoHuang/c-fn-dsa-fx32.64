Important validity notes:
- The floating B2A is only a 16-bit exponent conversion; the fixed B2A
  converts the complete 160-bit value. It is not a same-width comparison.
- masked_FALCON fails its GCC 13 CMake build under its own -Werror flags at
  gadgets.c:573-574. Clang -O2 was therefore used for both sides.
- UBSan aborts masked_FALCON at gadgets.c:161 because SecAdd shifts a uint64_t
  by 64. The timing above records the tested Clang build, not a UB-free claim.
- With AddressSanitizer enabled, the reference masked floating-point 5 * 3
  validation reconstructs the wrong result, even though the ordinary -O2 build
  passes that test. This sanitizer-sensitive result further limits confidence.
- Both random generators are demo-only and non-cryptographic. Their costs are
  included in operations that consume fresh masks.
- The current fixed-point repository has no masked floor, inverse/division,
  ApproxExp/BerExp, or SamplerZ peer. The corresponding floating figures are
  absolute baselines only and cannot be used to claim end-to-end speedup.
